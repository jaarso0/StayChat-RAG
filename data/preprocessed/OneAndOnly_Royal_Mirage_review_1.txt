Guest Review "” ONE&ONLY ROYAL MIRAGE ★★★★★

The most serene resort in Dubai. Unlike the flashy towers along Sheikh Zayed Road, this is a low-rise, Moorish-style property set in 65 acres of landscaped gardens.  Our Arabian Court room was elegant "” dark wood, arabesque patterns, a private balcony overlooking the palm-lined pools.

The one-kilometre private beach was uncrowded and pristine.   Breakfast at Olives was Mediterranean and fresh "” particularly good shakshuka and labneh. Complimentary WiFi was excellent.

The Oriental Hammam & Spa was the best spa experience we've ever had. Celebrities restaurant (fine dining) served outstanding French-Moroccan cuisine. Only downside: it's a 15-minute taxi to Dubai Mall. But that isolation is also its charm.  Highly recommended for couples.
