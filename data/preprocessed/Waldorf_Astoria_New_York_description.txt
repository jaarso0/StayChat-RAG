WALDORF ASTORIA NEW YORK

The Waldorf Astoria New York is a landmark Art Deco hotel on Park Avenue in midtown Manhattan.  Originally opened in 1931, it was for decades the world's largest and tallest hotel. The building is a designated New York City landmark.

The hotel features 1,413 rooms including the famous Presidential Suite (used by every sitting US President since Herbert Hoover) and the Waldorf Towers "” a luxury hotel-within-a-hotel occupying floors 27"”42.

The lobby is celebrated for its Art Deco grandeur: the "Wheel of Life" mosaic floor, the iconic four-sided bronze clock, and silver-and-gold coffered ceilings.  The Starlight Roof ballroom hosted legendary performances from Cole Porter to Frank Sinatra.
