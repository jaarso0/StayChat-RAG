BURJ AL ARAB "” DUBAI, UAE

Rising 321 metres above the Arabian Gulf on its own artificial island, the Burj Al Arab is widely regarded as one of the world's most luxurious hotels.  Designed by architect Tom Wright of WS Atkins, the sail-shaped silhouette has become an icon of modern Dubai.

The hotel features 202 duplex suites "” the smallest spanning 170 sqm and the Royal Suite stretching over 780 sqm. Every suite offers panoramic views of the Gulf through floor-to-ceiling windows. The interior's design blends Arabian opulence with contemporary luxury: marble floors, gold leaf accents, and bespoke furnishings.

The atrium lobby soars 180 metres high "” one of the tallest in the world "” featuring a dramatic fountain display and vibrant colour scheme.  Guests arrive via a dedicated bridge from the mainland, with Rolls-Royce transfers available as standard for suite bookings.
