The Hotel del Coronado (locally known as "˜The Del') is a historic beachfront resort in Coronado, California,  just across the bay from San Diego. Built in 1888, it is one of the few surviving examples of an American Victorian beach resort.

The original Victorian Building features the iconic red-roofed, white wooden architecture that has appeared in dozens of films, most famously "Some Like It Hot" (1959) starring Marilyn Monroe. The Crown Room "” with its stunning sugar-pine ceiling constructed without a single nail "” remains one of the hotel's architectural highlights.

In 2001 the property was expanded with the addition of The Shore, a contemporary oceanfront tower.   Today the resort offers 757 rooms and suites across three distinct accommodation styles.</
