Guest Review "” THE RITZ LONDON ★★★★★

Quintessential British luxury.  We booked the Deluxe King room and were upgraded to a Junior Suite overlooking Green Park. Afternoon tea in the Palm Court was magical "” finger sandwiches, warm scones with clotted cream, and a harpist playing in the background.

The Rivoli Bar serves exceptional cocktails. Staff were attentive without being intrusive.  Free WiFi was available but a bit slow during peak hours.

Breakfast included a full English with smoked salmon and scrambled eggs.   Location on Piccadilly is unbeatable "” walking distance to Buckingham Palace and the Royal Academy. The décor is Louis XVI style, gilded and elegant throughout.
